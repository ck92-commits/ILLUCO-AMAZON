## Why
The dental mirror listings have low star ratings and some negative reviews. That hurts customer confidence and conversion rate. The goal is to fix the dental mirrors first, then raise brand presentation and sales across the whole ILLUCO Amazon Store.

## Guardrails
- Get approval before major changes and before any ad-budget increase.
- Dental mirrors first, then every other active ASIN.
- Incentives must be Amazon-compliant: coupons, Prime-exclusive and lightning deals, S&S, promotions. No review incentives and no review manipulation.
- Specs and claims in listings must be accurate (materials, size, magnification, anti-fog, autoclavable, and so on). Check them against the physical product.
