# Amazon US Rules Checklist: ILLUCO Dental Mirror Revamp

**Researched:** Thursday 2026-11-05 (Amazon.com, US marketplace)
**Prepared for:** Cenk, ILLUCO Amazon lead
**Scope:** Titles, bullets, backend terms, images, A+, video, Store, reviews, promotions, Attribution, ads, and dental/medical-device compliance.

## How to read this

- Each rule ends with its source in brackets.
- **Primary** sources are Amazon, FDA, FTC, EPA or CPSC pages. **Secondary** sources are agency or tool blogs. Secondary sources are marked "secondary".
- Most Seller Central help pages need a login. We could not read them directly, so many Amazon rules come from Amazon forum posts (Amazon staff) or secondary blogs.
- **(unverified)** means we could not confirm the rule from a reliable source. Check it in Seller Central before acting.
- Policies change often. Two big changes landed in 2026: the **75-character title limit (July 27, 2026)** and **stricter reference prices (April 23, 2026)**.
- Project rule reminder: no major listing changes and no ad-budget increases until the manager reviews the initial findings (due Tue 11/10/2026).

## 1. Titles

**Current rule (since July 27, 2026)**

- Titles in all categories except media must be **75 characters or fewer, including spaces**. Amazon says the goal is to show the full title on mobile. [[Amazon Seller Forums, Amazon staff reply](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/96ae90ba-6fdf-4985-a0c4-52917ff5ab88)]
- Amazon announced the change in June 2026. July 27, 2026 is a "kick-off date, not a hard deadline". The rollout is gradual. [[Amazon Seller Forums, Amazon staff reply](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/878dffcd-ed3f-4570-88c8-497e547e825a)]
- A new **Item Highlights** field holds up to **125 characters**. Total capacity is still 200 characters (75 + 125). [[Amazon Seller Forums](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/878dffcd-ed3f-4570-88c8-497e547e825a)]
- Item Highlights show under the title in search and on the detail page (since about August 10, 2026). Amazon says both fields feed search, and neither is weighted more. [[EcomCrew summary of Amazon FAQ, secondary](https://www.ecomcrew.com/amazon-just-answered-the-big-questions-about-its-new-title-rules-heres-what-you-need-to-know/)]
- Titles that stay over 75 characters get **rewritten by Amazon's AI**. Brand owners get **14 days in "Review Listing Changes"** to review, edit or approve the AI version. Non-brand-registered sellers do not get this window. [[PPC Land, quotes Amazon notice, secondary](https://ppc.land/amazon-cuts-product-title-limit-to-75-characters-on-july-27/)]
- Keep the brand name in the title. Put the most important variation details (size, pack count) in the title and the rest in Item Highlights. [[EcomCrew, secondary](https://www.ecomcrew.com/amazon-just-answered-the-big-questions-about-its-new-title-rules-heres-what-you-need-to-know/)]
- Do not add Item Highlights to parent ASINs. Sellers report this causes errors. Some sellers also report error 100476 ("Item Highlight unsupported"). [[Amazon Seller Forums, seller reports](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/878dffcd-ed3f-4570-88c8-497e547e825a)]
- Official policy page (login needed): [[Seller Central GYTR6SYGFA5E3EQC](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/external/GYTR6SYGFA5E3EQC)]

**Rules from the January 21, 2025 title update (still listed as current by 2026 sources)**

- Do not use these characters unless they are part of the brand name: `! $ ? _ { } ^ ¬ ¦`. Pipes `|` and dashes `-` are allowed. [[My Amazon Guy, secondary](https://myamazonguy.com/news/2025-amazon-product-title-requirements/)] [[EcomCrew, secondary](https://www.ecomcrew.com/amazon-will-rewrite-your-product-titles-after-july-27/)]
- Do not repeat any word more than **twice** (the brand name counts). Prepositions, articles and conjunctions are exempt. Plurals and other forms of the same word count as repeats. [[My Amazon Guy, secondary](https://myamazonguy.com/news/2025-amazon-product-title-requirements/)] [[Search Engine Land via search summary, secondary](https://searchengineland.com/?p=450485)]
- No promotional phrases such as "free shipping", "best seller", "sale" or "100% quality guaranteed". [[EcomCrew, secondary](https://www.ecomcrew.com/amazon-will-rewrite-your-product-titles-after-july-27/)]
- Use numerals ("2 Pack", not "Two Pack") and abbreviate measurements. [[EcomCrew, secondary](https://www.ecomcrew.com/amazon-will-rewrite-your-product-titles-after-july-27/)]
- Category rules: no dental-specific title rule found. **(unverified)**
- ILLUCO note: Do not put "FDA Approved", "Antibacterial", "Sterile" or "Medical Grade" in titles or Item Highlights. See section 12.

## 2. Bullet points and product description

**Bullet points (updated rules since August 15, 2024)**

- Sellers get up to **5** bullets. Submit at least **3**. [[eComEngine, secondary](https://www.ecomengine.com/blog/amazon-bullet-point-guidelines)]
- Each bullet: more than **10** and fewer than **255** characters. Some sources say the field accepts 500 characters, so they conflict. Use 255 as the safe limit. [[eComEngine, secondary](https://www.ecomengine.com/blog/amazon-bullet-point-guidelines)] [[tool4seller, secondary](https://www.tool4seller.com/blog/updated_bullet_point_requirements_0815.html)]
- No special characters: ™, ®, ©, €, †, ‡, ¢, £, ¥, ±, ~ and similar. [[tool4seller, secondary](https://www.tool4seller.com/blog/updated_bullet_point_requirements_0815.html)]
- No emojis (✅, ❌, ☺ and similar). [[eComEngine, secondary](https://www.ecomengine.com/blog/amazon-bullet-point-guidelines)]
- **Restricted phrases:** "eco-friendly", "environmentally friendly", **"anti-microbial", "anti-bacterial"**, "made from bamboo", "contains soy". [[tool4seller, secondary](https://www.tool4seller.com/blog/updated_bullet_point_requirements_0815.html)] [[eComEngine, secondary](https://www.ecomengine.com/blog/amazon-bullet-point-guidelines)]
- No guarantee or refund language: "full refund", "if not satisfied send it back", "unconditional guarantee". [[tool4seller, secondary](https://www.tool4seller.com/blog/updated_bullet_point_requirements_0815.html)]
- No company info, website links, emails or phone numbers. [[eComEngine, secondary](https://www.ecomengine.com/blog/amazon-bullet-point-guidelines)]
- No ASINs or placeholder text ("N/A", "TBD", "COPY PENDING"). [[eComEngine, secondary](https://www.ecomengine.com/blog/amazon-bullet-point-guidelines)]
- No mention of other products outside this ASIN. No competitor comparisons. [[eComEngine, secondary](https://www.ecomengine.com/blog/amazon-bullet-point-guidelines)]
- Subjective, performance or comparative claims must be verifiable (for example on the packaging). Awards need a date and awarding body. Surveys need a source and date. [[eComEngine, secondary](https://www.ecomengine.com/blog/amazon-bullet-point-guidelines)]
- Format: start with a capital letter. Use sentence fragments with no end punctuation. Start with a short header and a colon. Spell out numbers one to nine (except measurements and model numbers). Put a space between a number and its unit ("2 mm"). No ALL CAPS. [[eComEngine, secondary](https://www.ecomengine.com/blog/amazon-bullet-point-guidelines)]
- Amazon may remove or rewrite non-compliant bullets, including with generative AI. [[tool4seller, secondary](https://www.tool4seller.com/blog/updated_bullet_point_requirements_0815.html)]

**Product description**

- Limit: **2,000 characters**. [[ChannelX, secondary](https://channelx.world/2021/04/amazon-html-ban-on-product-detail-pages/)]
- No HTML formatting since July 2021. Unsupported tags will not render. [[ChannelX, secondary](https://channelx.world/2021/04/amazon-html-ban-on-product-detail-pages/)]
- Product detail page rules: no links, no contact information, no reviews, no advertising. [[Seller Central: Product detail page rules, login needed](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/external/G202024200)]
- Once A+ Content is published, it usually replaces the plain description on the page. **(unverified)**

## 3. Backend search terms

- Stay **under 250 bytes**. If you go over, Amazon may ignore the whole field. [[WisePIM, quotes Amazon staff, secondary](https://wisepim.com/guides/amazon-backend-keywords)]
- Letters and digits are 1 byte. Accented letters and symbols can be 2 or 3 bytes. [[WisePIM, secondary](https://wisepim.com/guides/amazon-backend-keywords)]
- Do **not** include:
  - Your own brand or other brands' names (for example competitor dental brands). [[WisePIM, secondary](https://wisepim.com/guides/amazon-backend-keywords)]
  - ASINs. [[WisePIM, secondary](https://wisepim.com/guides/amazon-backend-keywords)]
  - Temporary statements ("new", "on sale now"). [[WisePIM, secondary](https://wisepim.com/guides/amazon-backend-keywords)]
  - Subjective claims ("best", "cheapest", "amazing"). [[WisePIM, secondary](https://wisepim.com/guides/amazon-backend-keywords)]
  - Profanity or abusive terms. [[WisePIM, secondary](https://wisepim.com/guides/amazon-backend-keywords)]
  - Repeated words, or words already in the title or bullets. Stop words and punctuation add nothing. [[WisePIM, secondary](https://wisepim.com/guides/amazon-backend-keywords)]
- Use lowercase words separated by single spaces. No commas needed. [[WisePIM, secondary](https://wisepim.com/guides/amazon-backend-keywords)]
- Include synonyms and alternate names ("mouth mirror", "dental inspection mirror", "oral mirror"). No need for misspellings. [[WisePIM, secondary](https://wisepim.com/guides/amazon-backend-keywords)]
- Prohibited keywords can lead to ASIN suppression. [[WisePIM, secondary](https://wisepim.com/guides/amazon-backend-keywords)]
- ILLUCO note: Pesticide-type words ("antibacterial", "kills germs") in backend terms can still trigger Amazon's pesticide filter. **(unverified)**

## 4. Product images

**Main image**

- Pure white background, **RGB 255, 255, 255**. [[Jungle Scout, secondary](https://www.junglescout.com/resources/articles/amazon-image-requirements/)]
- The product should fill about **85%** of the frame. [[Jungle Scout, secondary](https://www.junglescout.com/resources/articles/amazon-image-requirements/)]
- Real photo of the actual product. No drawings, mockups or placeholders. [[Jungle Scout, secondary](https://www.junglescout.com/resources/articles/amazon-image-requirements/)]
- No text, logos, borders, color blocks or watermarks on or behind the product. [[Jungle Scout, secondary](https://www.junglescout.com/resources/articles/amazon-image-requirements/)]
- No props or accessories that are not included in the sale. For a multi-pack, show only what the buyer gets. [[Jungle Scout, secondary](https://www.junglescout.com/resources/articles/amazon-image-requirements/)]
- One view only. Show the whole product with no cropping at the edge. Show it out of the packaging unless the case is a key feature. [[Jungle Scout, secondary](https://www.junglescout.com/resources/articles/amazon-image-requirements/)]

**All images**

- Size: at least **1,600 px** on the longest side is best for zoom. **1,000 px** is the minimum for zoom. 500 px is the bare minimum. Maximum is **10,000 px**. [[Jungle Scout, secondary](https://www.junglescout.com/resources/articles/amazon-image-requirements/)]
- Formats: **JPEG** (preferred), TIFF, PNG, GIF. No animated GIFs. [[Jungle Scout, secondary](https://www.junglescout.com/resources/articles/amazon-image-requirements/)]
- Color mode sRGB or CMYK. File name = product ID + extension (for example `B0XXXXXXX.MAIN.jpg`). **(unverified)**
- No Amazon logos, no "Amazon's Choice", "Best Seller" or "Top Seller" badges, and no look-alikes, in any image. [[Jungle Scout, secondary](https://www.junglescout.com/resources/articles/amazon-image-requirements/)]
- No blur, pixelation or jagged edges. [[Jungle Scout, secondary](https://www.junglescout.com/resources/articles/amazon-image-requirements/)]
- Count: upload up to **9** images. About 7 show on the page (6 if there is a video). [[Jungle Scout, secondary](https://www.junglescout.com/resources/articles/amazon-image-requirements/)]
- Secondary images may use infographics, text, lifestyle shots, size charts and comparison charts. The general rules still apply. [[Jungle Scout, secondary](https://www.junglescout.com/resources/articles/amazon-image-requirements/)]
- ILLUCO note: Never show the **FDA logo** in any image. Amazon prohibits it. [[Amazon forum post by Amazon moderator](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/f3750b36-67b8-4a34-b74a-e8fb14769f0d)]
- ILLUCO note: Infographic text must follow the same claim rules as the listing. No "antibacterial", "sterile" or "FDA approved" claims unless they are true and allowed (see section 12).

## 5. A+ Content (Basic and Premium) and Brand Story

**Eligibility**

- A+ requires Brand Registry. [[RiverBend Consulting, Apr 2026, secondary](https://riverbendconsulting.com/blog/amazon-a-plus-content/)]
- **Premium A+ is now open to all brand-registered Seller Central brand owners at no cost (May 2026).** The old rules (Brand Story on every ASIN plus a minimum number of approved A+ projects in 12 months) no longer apply. [[EcomCrew, quotes Amazon notice, secondary](https://www.ecomcrew.com/amazon-premium-a-content-is-now-free-for-all-brand-registered-sellers/)]
- Older rule for reference: Brand Story on all brand ASINs plus 5 (earlier 15) approved A+ projects in the last 12 months. [[Amazon Seller Forums EU](https://sellercentral-europe.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/cb36b705-3e9a-4c6f-92b0-398273ca66c6)]
- Confirm Premium access in A+ Content Manager. **(unverified on an Amazon page)**

**Prohibited content (Basic, Premium and Brand Story)**

- No pricing or promotional language ("free shipping", "sale", "discount"). [[RiverBend Consulting, secondary](https://riverbendconsulting.com/blog/amazon-a-plus-content/)] [[Awesome Dynamic A+ guide, secondary](https://www.awesomedynamic.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Amazon_A_Content_Creation_Guidelines_Awesome_Dynamic.pdf)]
- No shipping details. [[Awesome Dynamic, secondary](https://www.awesomedynamic.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Amazon_A_Content_Creation_Guidelines_Awesome_Dynamic.pdf)]
- No warranty or guarantee wording ("guaranteed results", "satisfaction guaranteed", "lifetime warranty"). Sellers report even brand "pledge" wording gets rejected. [[RiverBend Consulting, secondary](https://riverbendconsulting.com/blog/amazon-a-plus-content/)] [[Amazon Seller Forums](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/fe264878-1d2b-4202-bea7-21eb9342fa5a)]
- No customer reviews, review quotes, star ratings or press quotes. [[RiverBend Consulting, secondary](https://riverbendconsulting.com/blog/amazon-a-plus-content/)] [[Amazon KDP A+ help (books, same rule)](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G4WB7VPPEAREHAAD)]
- No competitor references or comparisons. [[Awesome Dynamic, secondary](https://www.awesomedynamic.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Amazon_A_Content_Creation_Guidelines_Awesome_Dynamic.pdf)]
- No time-sensitive words ("new", "latest", holiday references). [[Awesome Dynamic, secondary](https://www.awesomedynamic.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Amazon_A_Content_Creation_Guidelines_Awesome_Dynamic.pdf)]
- No superlatives you cannot prove ("#1", "best-selling", "top rated"). [[RiverBend Consulting, secondary](https://riverbendconsulting.com/blog/amazon-a-plus-content/)]
- No claims to treat medical conditions. [[Awesome Dynamic, secondary](https://www.awesomedynamic.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Amazon_A_Content_Creation_Guidelines_Awesome_Dynamic.pdf)]
- Eco claims need full proof. Awards must be from the last 2 years with the awarding body and date. Certifications must name the certifying body and year. [[Awesome Dynamic, secondary](https://www.awesomedynamic.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Amazon_A_Content_Creation_Guidelines_Awesome_Dynamic.pdf)]
- No external links, no off-Amazon customer service, no contact details. [[Awesome Dynamic, secondary](https://www.awesomedynamic.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Amazon_A_Content_Creation_Guidelines_Awesome_Dynamic.pdf)]
- Amazon checks text inside images too, not only text fields. [[search summary of A+ guides, secondary](https://myamazonguy.com/amazon/comprehensive-guide-amazon-aplus-content-faqs/)]

**Comparison charts**

- Compare **only your own ILLUCO products**. No competitor products. [[My Amazon Guy A+ FAQ, secondary](https://myamazonguy.com/amazon/comprehensive-guide-amazon-aplus-content-faqs/)]
- Only **one comparison chart per A+ page**. Amazon removes extra charts. [[eStore Factory, secondary](https://www.estorefactory.com/amazon-update/only-one-comparison-chart-allowed-per-a-content/)]
- Standard chart: 2 to 6 products, 1 to 10 comparison rows. [[Awesome Dynamic, secondary](https://www.awesomedynamic.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Amazon_A_Content_Creation_Guidelines_Awesome_Dynamic.pdf)]

**Alt text**

- Old rule: alt text up to 100 characters per image. [[Awesome Dynamic, secondary](https://www.awesomedynamic.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Amazon_A_Content_Creation_Guidelines_Awesome_Dynamic.pdf)]
- Amazon removed the alt-text field in some regions (Europe first, from 2025). Reports say AI now writes alt text. Whether the US field still exists today is **(unverified)**. [[eStore Factory, secondary](https://www.estorefactory.com/amazon-update/amazon-removes-alt-text-aplus-content/)]

**Images and mobile**

- Image files: JPG, BMP or PNG, RGB, under 2 MB, at least 72 dpi. Do not reuse your gallery images. [[Awesome Dynamic, secondary](https://www.awesomedynamic.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Amazon_A_Content_Creation_Guidelines_Awesome_Dynamic.pdf)]
- Most shoppers see A+ on mobile. Text inside images must be readable on a phone. Blurry or unreadable text gets rejected. [[Awesome Dynamic, secondary](https://www.awesomedynamic.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Amazon_A_Content_Creation_Guidelines_Awesome_Dynamic.pdf)]
- Premium A+ allows full-width layouts up to 1,464 px wide, video, hotspots, carousels and Q&A modules. [[EcomCrew, secondary](https://www.ecomcrew.com/amazon-premium-a-content-is-now-free-for-all-brand-registered-sellers/)]
- Review usually takes 3 to 7 business days. [[EcomCrew, secondary](https://www.ecomcrew.com/amazon-premium-a-content-is-now-free-for-all-brand-registered-sellers/)]

## 6. Videos

**Listing (detail page) videos**

- Eligible sellers can upload, often without Brand Registry, after about 3 months of selling. **(unverified on an Amazon page)** [[RevenueGeeks, Sep 2026, secondary](https://revenuegeeks.com/guides/amazon-product-video-requirements)]
- Format MOV or MP4. Up to 1080p. 16:9 or 9:16. Max **5 GB**. Length 1 to 12 minutes. Thumbnail JPEG/PNG/GIF/BMP under 5 MB. [[RevenueGeeks, secondary](https://revenuegeeks.com/guides/amazon-product-video-requirements)]
- No outside URLs, no promotional calls to action, no social-media watermarks, no unlicensed music or footage, no misleading claims. [[RevenueGeeks, secondary](https://revenuegeeks.com/guides/amazon-product-video-requirements)]
- Review takes 1 to 7 business days. [[RevenueGeeks, secondary](https://revenuegeeks.com/guides/amazon-product-video-requirements)]
- Pricing, review quotes, guarantees, competitor names, contact info and time-sensitive words are probably not allowed (same as A+). **(unverified)**

**Sponsored Brands Video (SBV) specs**

- Length **6 to 45 seconds**. 20 seconds or less is highly recommended. [[Amazon Ads SBV specs](https://advertising.amazon.com/resources/ad-specs/sponsored-brands-video)]
- Size 1280x720, 1920x1080 or 3840x2160. Aspect ratio **16:9 only**. [[Amazon Ads SBV specs](https://advertising.amazon.com/resources/ad-specs/sponsored-brands-video)]
- File **500 MB or less**. MP4 or MOV. H.264 or H.265 codec. Frame rate 23.976 to 30 fps. Bit rate at least 1 Mbps (4 Mbps+ recommended). Progressive scan. [[Amazon Ads SBV specs](https://advertising.amazon.com/resources/ad-specs/sponsored-brands-video)]
- Audio: PCM, AAC or MP3. Stereo or mono. At least 96 kbps and 44.1 kHz. [[Amazon Ads SBV specs](https://advertising.amazon.com/resources/ad-specs/sponsored-brands-video)]
- Videos autoplay **muted**. Show the product in the first few seconds. Use on-screen text so the message works without sound. Keep text out of the lower-right corner (mute button). [[Amazon Ads SBV specs](https://advertising.amazon.com/resources/ad-specs/sponsored-brands-video)]
- No letterboxing or pillarboxing. No black or blank frames at the start or end. No flashing or pulsing elements. [[Amazon Ads moderation guide](https://advertising.amazon.com/library/guides/sponsored-brands-display-ads-moderation)]
- Silent videos need a "No audio" note or on-screen text. Text and audio must be in English for the US. [[Amazon Ads moderation guide](https://advertising.amazon.com/library/guides/sponsored-brands-display-ads-moderation)]
- **No customer reviews or star ratings in video ads**, even real Amazon reviews. [[Amazon Ads moderation guide](https://advertising.amazon.com/library/guides/sponsored-brands-display-ads-moderation)]
- Sponsored Products video: MP4 or MOV, at least 7 seconds, max 500 MB, 1920x1080 recommended. [[RevenueGeeks, secondary](https://revenuegeeks.com/guides/amazon-product-video-requirements)]

## 7. Brand Store

- Requires a Professional seller account and a brand enrolled in **Brand Registry**. The Store is free. [[Sell on Amazon: Advertising](https://sell.amazon.com/advertising)]
- Hero image at least **3,000 x 600 px**, max 5 MB. Up to 30% can be cropped (15% per side). Keep key content in the middle. [[Amazon Ads Stores specs](https://advertising.amazon.com/resources/ad-specs/stores)]
- Logo at least **400 x 400 px**, max 5 MB. [[Amazon Ads Stores specs](https://advertising.amazon.com/resources/ad-specs/stores)]
- Up to **20 sections per page**. Max per page: 4 background videos, 1 product grid, 1 gallery, 1 featured deals, 1 recommended products. [[Amazon Ads Stores specs](https://advertising.amazon.com/resources/ad-specs/stores)]
- Image tiles: max 5 MB. Minimums: large 1,500 x 1,500, medium 1,500 x 750, small 750 x 750. 3,000 px wide recommended. [[Amazon Ads Stores specs](https://advertising.amazon.com/resources/ad-specs/stores)]
- Video tiles: MP4, H.264, cover image required. Full-width video at least 1,280 x 640. Background video 2 to 20 seconds, silent, no controls. [[Amazon Ads Stores specs](https://advertising.amazon.com/resources/ad-specs/stores)]
- Avoid text baked into images. Use the "Image with text" tile so text is readable by screen readers and search. Preview on mobile and desktop. [[Amazon Ads Stores specs](https://advertising.amazon.com/resources/ad-specs/stores)]
- Maximum number of Store pages: not found. **(unverified)**

**Prohibited Store content** (from Amazon's Stores content policy; the copy we could read is dated 2019, so check the current version)

- No email, web addresses, phone numbers, social links or QR codes. [[Amazon Stores content acceptance policy, 2019 copy](https://m.media-amazon.com/images/G/01/AmazonStores/Help/en/Content_acceptance_policy.html)]
- No competitor comparisons or putting down other brands. [[same](https://m.media-amazon.com/images/G/01/AmazonStores/Help/en/Content_acceptance_policy.html)]
- No specific prices or percentages. Promo messages only through the Featured Deals widget. No urgency wording. No exclamation points. No shipping claims. [[same](https://m.media-amazon.com/images/G/01/AmazonStores/Help/en/Content_acceptance_policy.html)]
- Claims must be true and backed up. Comparative claims need an independent source from the last 18 months. Awards must be less than 18 months old. [[same](https://m.media-amazon.com/images/G/01/AmazonStores/Help/en/Content_acceptance_policy.html)]
- Reviews: only real Amazon reviews, word-for-word, no reviewer names, verifiable on the detail page. No third-party ratings. [[same](https://m.media-amazon.com/images/G/01/AmazonStores/Help/en/Content_acceptance_policy.html)]
- Health: no claims to diagnose, cure, treat or prevent disease. Do not discourage seeing a professional. "Medical and OTC products" are a restricted category. [[same](https://m.media-amazon.com/images/G/01/AmazonStores/Help/en/Content_acceptance_policy.html)]
- No Amazon logos or trademarks. No fake buttons. [[same](https://m.media-amazon.com/images/G/01/AmazonStores/Help/en/Content_acceptance_policy.html)]

## 8. Customer reviews

**Never do this**

- No incentives for reviews: no free products, discounts, refunds, gift cards or rebates in exchange for a review. [[Jungle Scout, secondary](https://www.junglescout.com/blog/amazon-product-review-guidelines/)]
- Do not ask only happy customers. Do not send unhappy customers to email or support instead of reviewing. [[Jungle Scout, secondary](https://www.junglescout.com/blog/amazon-product-review-guidelines/)]
- Do not ask for a positive review. Do not ask a buyer to change or remove a review, even after you fix their problem. [[Jungle Scout, secondary](https://www.junglescout.com/blog/amazon-product-review-guidelines/)]
- No reviews by employees, family, friends or agencies. No reviewing competitors. No paid review services or review clubs. [[Jungle Scout, secondary](https://www.junglescout.com/blog/amazon-product-review-guidelines/)]
- No variation abuse (merging unrelated products to borrow ratings). [[Jungle Scout, secondary](https://www.junglescout.com/blog/amazon-product-review-guidelines/)]
- Do not misuse "Helpful" or "Report" buttons. [[Jungle Scout, secondary](https://www.junglescout.com/blog/amazon-product-review-guidelines/)]
- Product inserts must not ask for a positive or 5-star review, offer an incentive, or say "contact us instead of leaving a bad review". Neutral wording only. Sellers have been deactivated for inserts. [[Amazon Seller Forums, deactivation notice](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/247406a0-9f62-44a0-b28c-cc3d49008a35)] [[Jungle Scout, secondary](https://www.junglescout.com/blog/amazon-product-review-guidelines/)]
- Federal law too: the **FTC Consumer Reviews and Testimonials Rule (16 CFR Part 465)**, in force since **October 21, 2024**, bans fake reviews, buying reviews, incentives tied to a positive or negative sentiment, and review suppression. Courts can impose civil penalties. [[FTC Q&A](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/consumer-reviews-testimonials-rule-questions-answers)] [[FTC press release](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2024/08/federal-trade-commission-announces-final-rule-banning-fake-reviews-testimonials)]

**Allowed**

- **Request a Review button:** use it **5 to 30 days after delivery**. You cannot edit the message. [[eComEngine, secondary](https://ecomengine.com/blog/amazon-request-a-review-button)]
- Only **one** review request per order. Do not send both a button request and a buyer-seller message. [[eComEngine, secondary](https://ecomengine.com/blog/amazon-request-a-review-button)]

## 12. Dental and medical-device compliance (most important)

**FDA classification**

- A mouth mirror is a **Class I** device under **21 CFR 872.4565 (Dental hand instrument)**. "Mouth mirror" is named in the rule. [[Cornell LII: 21 CFR 872.4565](https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/21/872.4565)]
- FDA product code **EAX ("Mirror, Mouth")**, Dental panel. **510(k) exempt.** **GMP exempt: No.** [[FDA classification database: EAX](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/cdrh/cfdocs/cfpcd/classification.cfm?ID=EAX)]
- The exemption applies **only if the mirror is made of the same materials used before May 28, 1976**, and within the limits of **21 CFR 872.9**. [[Cornell LII: 872.4565](https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/21/872.4565)]
- 872.9 limits: a 510(k) is still needed if the device has a **different intended use** (for example, sold for lay users when existing devices are for professionals only) or uses a **different fundamental technology**. [[Cornell LII: 21 CFR 872.9](https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/21/872.9)]
- FDA has old 510(k)s for an "Electric Mouth Mirror" (1983), "Fiberglass Mouth Mirrors" (1984), anti-fog "Fogcutter" (1984), and consumer mirrors such as "Oravue by Water Pik" (1980). This shows LED, new-material and consumer mirrors have needed or used 510(k)s in the past. [[Innolitics FDA mirror of EAX, secondary](https://fda.innolitics.com/productcode/EAX.md)]
- **Action:** Have a regulatory consultant confirm the exemption for **LED** and **plastic-handle** models. A lighted mirror might also fall under a light product code. **(unverified)**
- Recognized standard: **ISO 9873:2019, Dentistry - Intra-oral mirrors.** [[FDA classification database: EAX](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/cdrh/cfdocs/cfpcd/classification.cfm?ID=EAX)]
- Even exempt Class I makers must **register the establishment** (and list the device). [[FDA classification database: EAX](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/cdrh/cfdocs/cfpcd/classification.cfm?ID=EAX)]
- Because GMP is not exempt, the maker must follow FDA's **QMSR (21 CFR 820, based on ISO 13485:2016)**, in force since **February 2, 2026**. [[FDA: QMSR](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/postmarket-requirements-devices/quality-management-system-regulation-qmsr)]
- Foreign manufacturers need a US agent. **(unverified)**

**Amazon medical-device policy**

- Class I and Class II non-exempt devices must be FDA cleared. If your product is exempt, **you must be able to show it is exempt**. [[Amazon forum post by Amazon moderator](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/f3750b36-67b8-4a34-b74a-e8fb14769f0d)] [[Seller Central: Medical devices policy, login needed](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/external/G200164650)]
- The device facility must be registered with FDA. No open FDA enforcement (for example warning letters). [[Amazon forum post by Amazon moderator](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/f3750b36-67b8-4a34-b74a-e8fb14769f0d)]
- No false or misleading statements on the page, packaging or inserts. [[same](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/f3750b36-67b8-4a34-b74a-e8fb14769f0d)]
- **Do not say "FDA cleared" or "FDA approved"** unless it is true. **Do not use the FDA logo.** [[same](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/f3750b36-67b8-4a34-b74a-e8fb14769f0d)]
- Prescription (Rx) devices may only be sold through Amazon's Professional Healthcare program and are not FBA-eligible. Mouth mirrors are normally not Rx. [[same](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/f3750b36-67b8-4a34-b74a-e8fb14769f0d)]
- "Professional use only" devices may be limited to licensed healthcare buyers on Amazon Business. This came from a forum post, not policy text. Wording like "For professional use only" could trigger this. **(unverified)**
- Whether Amazon gates a "Professional Dental Supplies" category or dental mirrors: **not found (unverified).** Check "Listing limitations apply" when adding each ASIN.

**Claims: what to remove or fix**

- **"FDA Approved":** Remove. Class I exempt mirrors are not "approved". [[Amazon forum post by Amazon moderator](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/f3750b36-67b8-4a34-b74a-e8fb14769f0d)]
- **"FDA Registered" / "FDA Certified":** Risky. Registration "does not in any way denote approval". Any wording that suggests approval because of registration is **misbranding**. [[21 CFR 807.39](https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/21/807.39)] FDA does not issue registration certificates. Showing them to imply approval misbrands the device. [[FDA Law Blog, secondary](https://www.thefdalawblog.com/2021/03/fda-warns-against-use-of-registration-certificates-it-dont-mean-a-thing/)]
- **"Antibacterial", "antimicrobial", "kills 99.9% of germs":** Remove unless you have the right registration and proof. Amazon treats these as **pesticide claims** and can remove the listing. You then need an EPA registration and establishment number, or an exemption. [[Amazon Seller Forums: pesticide listings](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/1a3229278a4d3401b392f1611d2f4311)] Amazon's bullet rules also restrict "anti-microbial" and "anti-bacterial". [[eComEngine, secondary](https://www.ecomengine.com/blog/amazon-bullet-point-guidelines)]
- **EPA treated-article rule:** A product with a built-in antimicrobial may claim only to protect **the product itself**. It must say clearly that it does not protect users from germs. No public-health claims ("kills pathogenic bacteria"). [[EPA PRN 2000-1](https://www.epa.gov/pesticide-registration/prn-2000-1-applicability-treated-articles-exemption-antimicrobial-pesticides)] [[EPA: Consumer products treated with pesticides](https://www.epa.gov/safepestcontrol/consumer-products-treated-pesticides)]
- **"Sterile":** Use only if sold sterile with a validated sterilization process. If only part of the product is sterile, say which part. Kits must not imply everything is sterile. [[FDA: Labeling Regulatory Requirements for Medical Devices](https://www.fda.gov/files/medical%20devices/published/Labeling---Regulatory-Requirements-for-Medical-Devices-(FDA-89-4203).pdf)] [[FDA: Labeling requirements for specific devices](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/general-device-labeling-requirements/labeling-requirements-specific-devices)]
- **"Autoclavable" / "reusable":** Only if tested. Devices the user sterilizes must list a validated method and cycle, and ideally the number of reuse cycles. LED handles with batteries usually **cannot** be autoclaved. Say exactly which parts can be autoclaved (for example "mirror head only"). [[FDA: Reprocessing guidance](https://www.fda.gov/media/80265/download)]
- Dentists treat mouth mirrors as **semicritical** items that must be heat-sterilized between patients. If a mirror cannot handle heat, CDC says to use a heat-tolerant or disposable item instead. Unclear reprocessing claims can drive professional complaints and bad reviews. [[CDC: Infection prevention in dental settings](https://www.cdc.gov/dental-infection-control/media/pdfs/2024/07/safe-care2.pdf)] [[CDC: Sterilization in dental settings](https://www.cdc.gov/dental-infection-control/hcp/dental-ipc-faqs/dental-sterilization.html)]
- **"Medical grade" / "surgical grade":** No official definition found. Use exact material grades instead (for example "stainless steel" plus the actual grade, "rhodium-coated front-surface mirror"). **(unverified)**
- **Health and diagnosis claims:** Do not claim to diagnose, treat or prevent disease ("detect cavities", "prevent gum disease"). This breaks Amazon content rules. For consumer models it may also change the FDA intended use (see 872.9). [[Amazon Stores content policy, 2019 copy](https://m.media-amazon.com/images/G/01/AmazonStores/Help/en/Content_acceptance_policy.html)] [[Cornell LII: 872.9](https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/21/872.9)]

**Battery-powered / LED mirrors**

- For FBA, you must give battery details: whether batteries are included or contained, composition (alkaline, lithium), type/size (for example LR44, CR2032), number of batteries and cells, weight, and lithium content (lithium metal) or watt-hours (lithium-ion). [[Amazon dangerous goods info doc (international version)](https://m.media-amazon.com/images/G/65/SG3P/Dangerous_goods_required_information_and_documentation_hazmat.pdf)] [[Seller Central G34151, login needed](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/external/G34151)]
- Amazon may ask for a **Safety Data Sheet (SDS) or a battery exemption sheet**. Complete data is usually reviewed within 2 business days. Incomplete data can block FBA sales. [[Amazon dangerous goods info doc](https://m.media-amazon.com/images/G/65/SG3P/Dangerous_goods_required_information_and_documentation_hazmat.pdf)]
- If inventory is already in a warehouse, you get **14 business days** to send documents, or the stock is disposed of at your cost. (This is from the international version of Amazon's doc. The US rule is probably similar.) [[same](https://m.media-amazon.com/images/G/65/SG3P/Dangerous_goods_required_information_and_documentation_hazmat.pdf)]
- **Reese's Law (button and coin cells, 16 CFR 1263 / UL 4200A):** It applies to consumer products with button or coin cells (secure battery door, warnings on product and packaging, certificate). CPSC says **medical devices are not "consumer products" and are not subject to Section 2**. [[CPSC Button Cell FAQs](https://www.cpsc.gov/FAQ/Button-Cell-and-Coin-Battery-FAQs)]
- But Amazon flags listings for "Consumer Button Cell Battery" compliance, sometimes by mistake. Be ready with proof: either the FDA device status, or UL 4200A test reports and a General Certificate of Conformity. **(unverified on an Amazon page)** [[Amazon Seller Forums, seller reports](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/23b774cd-e292-4974-b87e-d61cb0607680)]
- Spare button cells sold in the box need child-resistant packaging and warning labels. **(unverified for device kits)** [[CPSC Button Cell FAQs](https://www.cpsc.gov/FAQ/Button-Cell-and-Coin-Battery-FAQs)]

**California Prop 65**

- If any part contains a listed chemical (for example lead in metal parts, or certain plasticizers in handles) above safe levels, you need a Prop 65 warning. The seller is responsible. [[Seller Central: Prop 65, login needed](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/external/G202141960)]

- Ask suppliers for material test reports for handles, coatings and LED housings. **(recommendation)**
